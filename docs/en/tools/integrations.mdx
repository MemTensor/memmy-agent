In the workbench sidebar, open **Connections & Tools** and choose **App tools** at the top. This is where you connect documents, a calendar, or a collaboration service. What Memmy can use depends on the account and the access you authorize.

<details>
<summary>How are app tools, chat channels, and plugins different?</summary>

- **App tools**: let Memmy use an external service inside a task, such as reading pages in a connected Notion workspace.
- **Chat channels**: send Memmy a task from a messaging app such as Feishu or WeChat.
- **Plugins**: the tab is **Task plugins**. Memmy provides and maintains them, and installing one adds a complete capability to the current task. The cards show what is available and what each plugin does.

These entries are at the top of **Connections & Tools**. Pick the one for what you are doing now.

</details>

## Connect a service

The steps below use Notion. You can type another service name in the search box instead.

1. In **App tools**, search for `Notion`, then choose the result name or **+** on the right.
2. Read the connection notes and choose **Connect Notion**.
3. In the browser that opens, choose the workspace, check the access being requested, and finish authorization.
4. Back in Memmy, close the window after you see **Connected**. **+** in the list becomes **…**, which manages the connection.

## Use it in a task

Create a task on the workbench. Name the service, what to look through, and the result you want. For example:

<PromptExample composer={true} prompt="In the connected Notion workspace, find pages whose titles contain “Team training”. List each title and where it lives, ordered by last edited time, and pull out the to-dos that are not finished. Only read those pages. Do not create or change anything." />

Check the page titles, locations, and to-dos in the result. If there is no matching page, it should say so. If it used the wrong workspace, add the correct name and continue.

## View or disconnect

In **App tools**, choose **Connected**, then **…** on the right of the service. The window shows the connection status. When you no longer need it, choose **Disconnect**. The card returns to **+**, and the service leaves the **Connected** list.

## If the connection fails

<details>
<summary>The browser did not open, or it keeps waiting</summary>

In Memmy’s waiting window, choose **Reopen browser**, finish sign-in and confirmation on the authorization page, then return to Memmy. If the list has not updated, choose the refresh icon at the top right.

<ProductScreenshot src={waitingScreen} alt="Full Memmy window waiting for Notion authorization, with Reopen browser" caption="Authorization happens in the browser. A waiting state does not mean the connection succeeded." />

If it says the connection timed out or the service is temporarily unavailable, close the notice and connect again from the service card later.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says connected, but the content is missing</summary>

Confirm you authorized the right account, and check in the original service that this account can open the material. In the question, add the page name, where it lives, or a date range. If it still fails, open the manage window, connect again, and check the authorized scope.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The service is not in search</summary>

Switch back to **All**, set **Tool category** to **All categories**, clear the search, and look through the catalog. Only services in the current catalog can be connected here.

</details>
