In the workbench sidebar, open **Connections & Tools** and choose **Chat channels** at the top. After a channel is connected, you can send Memmy a task from that messaging app.

## Connect a channel

Choose the channel name or **+** on the right, then follow the window. WeChat, Feishu, and DingTalk are labeled WeChat, Feishu, and DingTalk.

<div className="docs-actions-table">

| Channel | How to connect |
| --- | --- |
| **WeChat** | Scan the connection QR code with WeChat. |
| **Feishu** | Scan to create and connect a bot. If you already have an app, enter the App ID and App Secret instead. |
| **DingTalk** | Enter the Client ID and Client Secret of a custom app. |
| **Telegram** | Enter the bot token from BotFather. |
| **Discord** | Enter the bot token of a bot you created. |
| **iMessage** | Connect the Messages app on this Mac, and turn on the system permissions it asks for. |

</div>

## Connect Feishu once

1. Choose the **Feishu** card, then **Scan to create and connect**.
2. Scan the QR code with the Feishu app, read the prompt, and confirm creating and connecting the app.
3. Return to Memmy. If needed, choose **I've scanned** and wait until the status is **Connected**.
4. In Feishu, open the chat with that bot, send a task, and confirm a reply comes back.

<details>
<summary>Enter Feishu, DingTalk, or bot credentials yourself</summary>

- **Feishu**: choose **Already have a Feishu app? Enter App ID / Secret**, enter the credentials, then choose **Connect Feishu**. Follow the window to grant messaging and card permissions, then create and publish an app version.
- **DingTalk**: open the DingTalk card, enter the Client ID and Client Secret, and choose **Connect DingTalk**.
- **Telegram / Discord**: open the card, enter the bot token, and choose the connect button.

Each channel window includes a guide for getting the credentials. After it connects, return to the list, confirm **Connected**, and send a task to that bot.

<details>
<summary>Connect iMessage</summary>

iMessage uses the Messages app on this Mac. The connection window has **Open Full Disk Access** and **Open Automation**. One is for receiving messages, and the other lets Memmy control Messages to send them. Finish the permissions as the window directs, then return to Memmy and connect.

</details>

## Send a task

Keep the computer running Memmy online. In the connected messaging app, send Memmy a message and say what you want done. If no reply arrives, check the channel status, then read the error on the card.

## Manage the connection

In **Chat channels**, choose **Connected**, then **…** on the right of a channel to see its status. To stop using it, choose **Disconnect** in the window. That does not delete tasks or memory already on the workbench.

<details>
<summary>Feishu says connected, but some features do not work</summary>

Open the Feishu channel’s manage window and see whether it says the bot does not have enough permission. Add the permissions it lists, create and publish an app version, then send the task again and check the result.

</details>
