Memmy is an AI desktop assistant with long-term memory. You can hand it a task, or bring in conversations from other AI tools, so later work can use that background.

## Continue the same work across AIs

An arrangement discussed in one AI can be organized into Memmy memory, then found again by a connected AI. For example, ask Codex to organize meeting notes, then ask Deepseek to draft a to-do list and share it with others.

You choose whether new conversations keep being collected, and whether connected AIs automatically look up related memory.

## Find what you just did

After you turn on **Memory → Computer History**, Memmy summarizes activity from the apps and websites you allow. You can ask “what was that planning file I looked at today” or “what is still undecided for tomorrow’s meeting”. Recording starts only after you turn it on, and you can pause or delete it at any time.

## Finish a specific task

Give Memmy a goal and the materials it needs: organize meeting notes, compare web pages, draft a training notice, or create a document or spreadsheet. With permission, it can also use the browser and desktop apps. After a result arrives, keep revising it in the same task.

**Recording** on the workbench can record or upload audio, transcribe it, and pull out the points. Repeated reminders and checks can be scheduled.

## Keep materials and methods

A project keeps related tasks with a local folder. A knowledge base stores material you need to look up again. A skill stores a method you can reuse. Memory helps a new task recover past conversations, preferences, and decisions.

## Connect tools when you need them

In **Connections & Tools** you can connect external services, connect a messaging app, or install a plugin. What is available depends on your account, model, platform, and the permissions you grant. The desktop pet is a small entry point for asking something at any time.
