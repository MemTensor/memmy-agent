The Office plugin creates or revises Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF files.

## Install

1. On the left, open **Connections & Tools** and choose **Task plugins**.
2. Find the Office card and choose **+**. This plugin does not require sign-in.
3. After it is installed, choose **Try it**, or open **View details** and choose **Add to chat**.

<ProductScreenshot src={catalogScreen} alt="Task plugins page with Task plugins selected, showing Literature Review and Office" caption="Example of where to find the cards. Both cards appear uninstalled here; the installation state in your app may differ. The English card title is Office." />

## The first run or an export

The first time you create, export, or convert a file, the app prepares a verified runtime. You do not install that runtime yourself. If an export is still waiting or fails, go back to **Connections & Tools → Task plugins** and check this card. Try again after preparation finishes.
