The desktop pet is Memmy’s small window. Choose it to type a request, or to see task progress and unread replies.

## Open the pet

Choose the Memmy icon in the system menu bar or tray, then **Pet mode**. After the pet appears, drag it to move it.

## Start or continue a chat

Choose the pet, type in **What do you wanna say?**, and choose the send arrow. After a reply, keep typing below it. When the reply is long, choose **Open full conversation**.

## See tasks and unread replies

The number beside the pet is the unread session count. Choose the number to open recent tasks, then choose a task to read its reply. A running task also has a progress hint at the top.

While a task is running, the bubble offers **New chat** and **Stop**. **New chat** starts another topic. **Stop** stops the current task.

## Switch to the full workbench

Double-click the pet, or choose **Back to full mode** in the right-click menu. **Open full conversation** in a reply bubble also opens the workbench on that task.

**Close pet** in the right-click menu hides the small window. Memmy keeps running in the background. Choose **Pet mode** again from the menu bar or tray icon when you want it back.

## Change how Memmy starts

Open **Settings → Preferences**, then the startup and window section. Under **Default launch mode**, choose pet mode. Under **When closing the main window**, choose what should happen, such as shrinking to the pet.
