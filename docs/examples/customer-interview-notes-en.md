# Customer interview notes (practice file)

The interviews, people, and situations below are fictional and are only for the documentation exercise.

## Interview A: Getting started

Quote: “The first time I entered the system, I was not sure whether to create a project or add files first. I wanted someone to tell me the first step.”

## Interview B: Finding material

Quote: “The same handbook has several versions. I often spend time checking which one is current.”

## Interview C: Working as a team

Quote: “Two colleagues sometimes reply to the same customer question. We want to know who is following up now.”

## What these notes do not say

These notes do not say how often the problem happens, how many people it affects, an acceptable price, or why a customer left. If an explanation of those points is proposed, mark it as to be verified. It is not an interview fact.
